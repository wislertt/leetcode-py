- E3 parallel subagent spawn (6 concurrent) | validated + closed | 2026-09-01 | With zero shared-file writes confirmed (E1) and p-gen scoped to its own problem dir, raising 3→6 should roughly halve wall time per problem; agents are mostly model latency + I/O, test bursts are short | (a) zero file/race failures or boundary violations, (b) wall/problem clearly under the 3-concurrent ~67-103s baseline, (c) no gate degradation vs 3-concurrent runs | PARTIAL: (a) and (c) pass clean — 0 races, 0 violations, all gates first-try PASS. (b) FAILS as measured: ~65s/problem wall ≈ the 3-concurrent baseline, not half. Cause analysis: batch started at 3-4 concurrency (~10 min), and respawn dead time (notification → orchestrator turn → spawn, ~1-3 min per report, one at a time) dominates at higher concurrency. Agent durations (~83-333s, avg ~180s) did not inflate vs 3-concurrent → CPU cap not reached at 6. Verdict: 6 is safe but wall-time gain is eaten by orchestrator respawn latency; the lever is batched respawns, not raw concurrency. Keep 6; test 8 only alongside a batching change (E4).
- E4 reconcile-to-target spawns (default 8 concurrent) | validated + closed | 2026-09-01 | E3 showed one-for-one respawning (spawn 1 per notification) lags 1-3 min per report and let the flight sag to 3-4, eating the concurrency gain; holding the flight at a target by counting running agents each turn and spawning the deficit at once should keep 8 agents nearly always in flight and cut wall/problem well below the ~65s E3 mark | (a) flight stays >=6 most of the batch (short 1-2 dips acceptable), (b) wall/problem clearly under ~65s, (c) zero races/violations/gate failures, (d) agent durations not inflated vs E3 ~180s avg | VALIDATED in batch 14: (a) flight held at 6-8 after the initial ramp, only single-turn dips of 1-2 (recovered by deficit spawns next turn), (b) ~26-30s/problem wall (2x better than the ~65s E3 mark; avg agent duration ~214s over a ~7.5 flight), (c) all gates PASS first try, 0 races, 0 violations, (d) avg 214s vs E3 ~180s — within noise, longest agents (399s, 454s) were genuinely harder problems, not CPU contention. Reconcile-to-target spawning is the mechanism that made 8-concurrent pay; keep 8 as default.
